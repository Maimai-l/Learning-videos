# Script

The video should leave the student with two things: understanding the concept, and being able to write what earns the marks. They are different. Understanding comes from seeing why, ideally to the point where the student feels they could have found it themselves. Marks come from the exact statements and steps the mark schemes accept. Explain for understanding in your own way; when the narration states what the student must write, use the plan's wording.

Everything comes from the lesson plan. Do not add content from outside it.

Narrate in the student's language, with terms in the plan's `answer_language` (give the student-language equivalent the first time). `say` is read by a speech model, so mathematics and symbols are written as they are spoken.
